## 📋 How to Use

### Step 1: Choose Your Mode
- Click **📄 Score Sheet** for image/PDF scanning
- Click **🎵 Audio File** for audio chord detection (unchanged)

### Step 2: Upload Your File
- **Drag & drop** your file onto the dropzone, OR
- **Click "Choose File"** to browse

### Step 3: Check Quality (Images Only)
- After selecting an image, you'll see a **Quality Analysis Card**
- **Green** = Excellent quality (300+ DPI)
- **Orange** = Acceptable quality (200-300 DPI)
- **Red** = Poor quality (<200 DPI) - consider re-scanning

### Step 4: Scan
- Click the **"Scan"** button
- Watch the professional scanning animation:
  - Moving scan line
  - Progress bar (0-100%)
  - Stage-by-stage updates
  - Estimated time remaining

### Step 5: View Results
- Chords appear with smooth animations
- Each chord fades in sequentially
- Success indicator confirms completion
- Download PDF option available

## 💡 Tips for Best Results

### For PNG/JPG Images:
- ✅ Use **300-400 DPI** resolution
- ✅ Scan in **grayscale or black & white** mode
- ✅ Ensure **good lighting** (no shadows)
- ✅ Keep the page **straight**
- ✅ Use **printed music** (not handwritten)
- ✅ Make sure **staff lines are clear**

### For PDF Files:
- ✅ Digital PDFs work great
- ✅ Clean scanned PDFs are automatically optimized
- ✅ Multi-page PDFs supported

### What to Avoid:
- ❌ Blurry or out-of-focus images
- ❌ Heavily skewed pages
- ❌ Low-contrast scans
- ❌ Handwritten music
- ❌ Photos with shadows
- ❌ Very low resolution (<200 DPI)

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### "Could not extract notation"
**Causes:**
- Image quality too low
- Not a valid music score
- Staff lines not detected
- Handwritten music

**Solutions:**
1. Check the quality analysis before scanning
2. Re-scan at higher resolution (400+ DPI)
3. Ensure staff lines are clearly visible
4. Try converting to PDF first
5. Use a document scanner instead of phone camera

### "Low resolution detected"
**Causes:**
- Image taken with phone at low quality
- Screenshot of music
- Heavily compressed JPEG

**Solutions:**
1. Use a proper scanner at 300-400 DPI
2. If using a phone, use document scanning apps
3. Avoid screenshots - scan the original
4. Save as PNG instead of JPEG

### Animation Not Smooth
**Causes:**
- Browser performance issues
- Too many tabs open
- Older device

**Solutions:**
1. Close unnecessary browser tabs
2. Use Chrome or Edge for best performance
3. Restart your browser
4. Disable browser extensions temporarily

### Quality Levels

**🟢 Excellent (300+ DPI)**
- Perfect for scanning
- Best chord detection accuracy
- Proceed with confidence

**🟠 Acceptable (200-300 DPI)**
- Should work reasonably well
- Consider re-scanning for better accuracy
- May miss some details

**🔴 Poor (<200 DPI)**
- High risk of detection failure
- Strongly recommend re-scanning
- Not ideal for accurate results
